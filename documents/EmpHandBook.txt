==================================================

Employees must:

- Maintain respectful communication.
- Avoid harassment and discrimination.
- Protect company property.
- Follow workplace safety guidelines.


Unacceptable Behavior:

- Bullying
- Harassment

6. WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
==================================================

Standard Working Hours:

Working Days: Monday to Friday

Working Hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM

Break Time: 1 Hour


Employees must:

- Report on time.
- Inform managers about delays or absences.
- Maintain attendance records.

==================================================

Employees are eligible for:

management system.
