1. INTRODUCTION
==================================================

The Leave Policy of ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. defines the rules
and guidelines for employee leave management.

This policy ensures that employees can take necessary time off
while maintaining smooth business operations.


==================================================
2. PURPOSE OF LEAVE POLICY
==================================================

The purpose of this policy is to:

- Provide clear guidelines for requesting and approving leave.
- Support employee health, personal needs, and work-life balance.
- Maintain proper attendance and workforce planning.


==================================================
3. TYPES OF LEAVE
==================================================


1. CASUAL LEAVE (CL)

Purpose:
Casual Leave is provided for personal work, emergencies, or short
periods of absence.

Eligibility:
All full-time employees.

Leave Allowance:
12 Days per year.


--------------------------------------------------


2. SICK LEAVE (SL)

Purpose:
Sick Leave is provided when an employee is unable to work due to
health-related issues.

Eligibility:
All employees after completing company requirements.

Leave Allowance:
10 Days per year.

Medical proof may be required for extended sick leave.


--------------------------------------------------


3. PAID LEAVE (PL)

Purpose:
Paid Leave allows employees to take planned vacations or personal
time off while receiving regular salary.

Leave Allowance:
15 Days per year.


--------------------------------------------------


4. MATERNITY LEAVE

Purpose:
Provided to eligible employees for maternity-related requirements.

Duration:
As per applicable company rules and government regulations.


--------------------------------------------------


5. PATERNITY LEAVE

Purpose:
Provided to eligible employees after the birth of a child.

Duration:
As per company policy.


==================================================
4. LEAVE ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

Employees must:

- Be registered as active employees.
- Follow the leave application procedure.
- Obtain approval before taking planned leave.


==================================================
5. LEAVE APPLICATION PROCESS
==================================================

Employees must:

1. Submit leave requests through the company leave management
   system.

2. Mention:
   - Leave type
   - Start date
   - End date
   - Reason for leave

3. Wait for manager approval before taking leave.


==================================================
6. LEAVE APPROVAL GUIDELINES
==================================================

Managers will approve leave based on:

- Business requirements.
- Employee workload.
- Team availability.
- Previous leave records.


==================================================
7. LEAVE RULES AND REGULATIONS
==================================================

- Leave should be requested in advance whenever possible.
- Unauthorized absence may result in disciplinary action.
- Employees must complete pending tasks before planned leave.
- Leave balance will be maintained by the HR department.
- Leave cannot affect critical business activities.


==================================================
8. EMERGENCY LEAVE POLICY
==================================================

In case of emergencies:

- Employees must inform their reporting manager immediately.
- Leave approval will be handled based on the situation.
- Supporting documents may be requested if required.


==================================================
9. HOLIDAY POLICY
==================================================

Employees are entitled to company-declared holidays.

Types of holidays:

- National holidays
- Festival holidays
- Company holidays


The annual holiday calendar will be released by the HR department.


==================================================
10. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
==================================================

Leave Without Pay applies when:

- Employee has exhausted available leave balance.
- Additional leave is approved without paid leave availability.

Salary deductions will be applied based on the number of unpaid
leave days.


==================================================
11. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES
==================================================

Employees must:

- Apply for leave honestly.
- Maintain proper communication with managers.
- Complete assigned responsibilities.
- Follow company leave procedures.
